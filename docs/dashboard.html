Method in one paragraph

2,000 unique images (1,000 AI / 1,000 real) after removing 1,998 exact duplicates, split once into 1,400 / 300 / 300 (stratified, seed 42) and frozen in a hash manifest. Every image is centre-cropped to a square, re-encoded as JPEG q95 and stripped of metadata so file format, aspect ratio and EXIF no longer reveal the label. Models were trained on the training split only, checkpoints and the deployed model were chosen on the validation split, and the test split was evaluated once. Full details: report.
